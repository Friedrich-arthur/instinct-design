# Sources and boundaries

Reviewed on 2026-10-13. This is an independent design synthesis, not an official Instinct publication or a claim of endorsement.

## Public product introduction

[Instinct's official site](https://instinct.com/) describes a personal assistant that understands the user's context and can be contacted through text or calls. The page was read during preparation of this version. Its phrase "there are no new interfaces" supports familiar entry points. The site does not establish a complete message protocol or brand system.

## Interview inspiration

[Public interview video associated with Noah Shinn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Am7IWP8IpEc) is the source linked by the author's research notes. The inherited notes describe understandability, predictable behavior, information near the beginning of a message, contextual capability, progressive trust, and selective interruption.

The notes were summarized from machine-recognized material. The original transcript was not recovered or checked line by line for this release. The video URL and page title were checked, but that does not verify every summarized claim. No verbatim founder quotation, precise interview timestamp, product usage metric, or investment figure is used as evidence of this Skill's effectiveness.

## The author's method

Outcome-first communication, plain language, a quiet primary view, and depth on demand are the author's synthesis of the research and product-design goals. The message patterns, three-level interaction model, visual defaults, review questions, and synthetic examples are authored applications.

The method does not require a fixed message length, a specific palette, a maximum number of controls, or a universal chat interface. Those decisions depend on the user and task. No claim is made that Instinct implements every recommendation in this repository.

## Packaging and evaluation

- [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification) defines the folder and progressive loading conventions.
- [Vercel Skills CLI](https://github.com/vercel-labs/skills) documents installation and agent selection.
- [Agent Skills evaluation guide](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/evaluating-skills) informs task-based comparison with and without a Skill.

These sources concern packaging and evaluation, not Instinct's product philosophy. Installation counts, GitHub Stars, agent judgments, and customer outcomes are different measures.
