## One improvement and its recheck condition

The Chinese discovery entry linked to an English full teardown. We added a complete Chinese guide with the same evidence boundaries, plus a worksheet and expert-table counterexample. It now links from the Chinese overview and the README. A generator keeps the article body aligned with the installed reference.

The observed gap justifies this language-access improvement. It does not forecast traffic. Recheck after the first permitted Chinese design-task report. Ask whether the reader found the relevant pattern, produced a usable artifact, and understood when the pattern would fail. If a reader cannot move from the article to a task, fix that transition before adding more channels.

## Collect feedback without a new platform

Use the repository's [design feedback issue form](https://github.com/Friedrich-arthur/instinct-design/issues/new/choose). Record the entry URL or source reported by the person, the sanitized task, actual output, practical consequence, environment, and permission to publish. Record later reuse only when that person reports it or permitted evidence shows it.

Review directory status and repository metrics manually when useful feedback arrives. Check the body of any claimed directory listing. Use authorized search tooling when available; keep missing metrics marked unavailable. Close [the distribution task](https://github.com/Friedrich-arthur/instinct-design/issues/5) and [adoption review](https://github.com/Friedrich-arthur/instinct-design/issues/6) only after their external evidence exists.
