# A useful lesson before the installation link

Prepared 2026-10-13. These are publication-ready drafts, not posted content or measured traffic. Replace a draft only with an actual result when a public case becomes available. Keep the independent, unaffiliated status visible.

## Main promise

Improve your own product with Instinct-inspired design philosophy. Bring a screen, flow, spec, or code; get a concrete change and a way to check it, then iterate from real feedback. The evidence-backed teardown explains the inspiration when needed.

Choose one hook per publication:

- A video can be exported while its post is still unpublished. Design a message that preserves both facts.
- Disconnecting an integration does not necessarily delete saved information. Design a receipt the user can understand.
- A proactive assistant needs to know when the user can still prevent a consequence. Try the three-condition attention exercise.

These hooks teach authored design applications. They do not demonstrate the private Instinct service or claim an improvement percentage.

## LinkedIn: a product-design lesson

> One lesson from researching Instinct is that access, retained data, and external actions need separate product states.
>
> The public privacy policy distinguishes disconnecting an integration from deleting previously collected data. That becomes a practical design exercise: show what stopped, what remains, and which effects already happened.
>
> I packaged this and seven other patterns into an independent AI product-design Skill. The teardown separates official claims, interview-note interpretations, and our own design proposals.
>
> It includes a small attention exercise and copyable prompts. Try one flow and share where the pattern fails.
>
> https://friedrich-arthur.github.io/instinct-design/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=instinct_v02

The source for the policy lesson is https://instinct.com/privacy-policy. Keep that link available when sharing the claim.

## 中文长文：完整文章与可用入口

[完整中文文章](https://friedrich-arthur.github.io/instinct-design/zh/guide/)已写出八个模式、证据边界、反例、借鉴卡和三个练习。可在适合的社区分享原链接；发布到其他平台时遵守账号授权与平台规则。它由 Skill 内原文生成。

标题建议：**从 Instinct 借鉴 AI 产品设计：8 个模式，3 个实践练习**

开头：

> AI 产品能调用很多工具，用户仍可能不知道任务是否完成。借鉴 Instinct 时，我最关心的是怎样把后台能力变成前台可理解的结果。这篇拆解把每个观点落到依据、机制假说、应用条件、反例和检查方法。

正文依次展开：熟悉入口、可预测结果、消息预览、按需界面、权限与数据、主动打扰、目标约束、品味与实测。用仓库中的原创建议和合成练习，不粘贴完整访谈或私人笔记。重点例子是“导出成功但上传失败”和“断开后仍保留数据”。

结尾：

> 先选一个模式，改一条消息或一个流程，再让真实使用者判断能否看懂结果和下一步。文档、截图和安装成功各有证据边界，真实改善需要实际任务反馈。
>
> 中文深拆：https://friedrich-arthur.github.io/instinct-design/zh/guide/?utm_source=article&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=instinct_v03

新的短分享开头可用：“对 Instinct 感兴趣，可以先带走一个具体设计思路。比如账号断开后，未来访问、保留数据和已发生的外部行动要分别交代。我把这些证据与迁移条件写成八个模式，附可直接填写的借鉴卡。”只选择一个主链接，先给读者价值，再引导安装。
